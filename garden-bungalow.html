Our garden bungalows are standalone timber buildings set on traditional stilts, featuring their own private verandas set back among the palms. They offer a peaceful retreat while remaining within earshot of the waves and just a short stroll from the restaurant and dive centre.

Each bungalow fits up to two adults comfortably, with options to add an extra bed for small families or triple-share arrangements. Constructed from local Sulawesi hardwood, the natural air circulation keeps the interiors naturally cool alongside ceiling fans.
