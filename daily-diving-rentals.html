Diving & Equipment Pricing

Welcome day-trippers and guests: You do not have to stay here to do your diving or rent gear with us! Feel free to join our boat trips or rent equipment directly from our dive centre.

Planning to stay on the island? Booking your dives as an accommodation & dive package works out cheaper than paying individual daily rates. Check out our Dive Package Rates on the home page.
