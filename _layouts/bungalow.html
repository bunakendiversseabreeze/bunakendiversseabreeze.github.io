What's Included in Your Stay

All room rates operate on a full-board basis. Your nightly booking rate includes:

  • Full cooked breakfast, lunch, and dinner daily in our open-air restaurant.
  • Unlimited fresh drinking water, tea, and brewed local coffee available 24/7.
  • Private ensuite bathroom with a Western toilet and shower.
  • Daily housekeeping and linen change service.

Check-in is available from 2:00 PM, and checkout is until 12:00 PM. If you are arriving early on the morning boat, you are welcome to drop off gear and enjoy a meal while your room is prepared.
