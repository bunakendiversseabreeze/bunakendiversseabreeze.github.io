The Resort

Built from the same island you're diving

Every bungalow at Seabreeze is raised timber-on-stilts, built by local builders from Sulawesi hardwood, set within fifty metres of the water's edge. There's no pool — the pool is offshore and has better visibility.

Everything you need, right on your doorstep — without the feel of a big resort. With just a small number of cosy bungalows, a private beach, an onsite restaurant and our own dive centre, you can enjoy a relaxed, intimate stay where everything is close by. Family-run by locals, we offer a genuinely personal experience, with the warmth and charm of the Bunaken island lifestyle.

Bunaken's wall is famous for a reason: it drops from the shallows to well past recreational limits, in a marine park that has protected it since 1991. We keep our groups small, our boats close to the house reef, and our dive guides local — most of them grew up diving this exact wall.

Getting here

We're on Pangalisang Beach, Bunaken — a 40-minute boat ride from Manado Harbour, which is itself about a half-hour drive from Manado (Sam Ratulangi) International Airport. Transfers can be arranged for an additional charge.

Where we dive

30+ sites, a short boat ride away

We run trips to more than thirty sites around Bunaken, Manado Tua, Siladen, and Manterawu — a mix of dramatic wall dives, gentler slopes, muck diving, and more. The guides pick the day's sites around conditions and your interests.

  • Wall divesreef sharks, turtles, big schools of fusiliers
  • Slopes & reefhard & soft coral gardens, mandarinfish at dusk
  • House reef & muckfrogfish, nudibranchs, macro life
  • Current sitespelagics, schooling barracuda

…and many more.

Upon special arrangement and depending on availability, we can also arrange trips further afield to Bangka or the Lembeh Strait — both well worth the extra distance for divers after something different.

Other activities

Dolphin swimming tours, island exploration & snorkelling

Beyond scuba diving, there are plenty of ways to experience Bunaken — whether you want an early morning offshore adventure, land exploration, or a relaxed swim right off the beach.

Dolphin Swimming Tours

Head out on an early morning boat trip to meet Bunaken’s wild dolphins in open water. Watch them leap along the bow or slip into the water for an unforgettable swim with our cetacean friends.

Rp500,000 / person

Rent a moped to explore Bunaken Island at your own pace. Discover local villages, quiet viewpoints, and coastal spots beyond the resort.

Boat Snorkelling Trips

Join our dive boat excursions to explore the wider marine park. Heading out by boat gives you access to far more varied and vibrant reef sites than staying near the shore.
