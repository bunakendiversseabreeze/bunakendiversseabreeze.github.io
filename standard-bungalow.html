Our Standard Bungalow is designed as a four-room timber building set on traditional stilts, sharing a wide, covered veranda overlooking our tropical garden pathways. Rooms can be booked individually or together by groups.

Each room fits up to two adults comfortably, with options to add an extra bed for small families or triple-share arrangements. Constructed from local Sulawesi hardwood, the natural air circulation keeps the interiors naturally cool alongside ceiling fans.
