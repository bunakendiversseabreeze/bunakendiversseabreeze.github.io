Our seafront bungalows are standalone timber buildings raised on traditional stilts right at the water's edge. Enjoy unobstructed ocean views directly from your private veranda—you can wake up to the sight of the waves and walk straight down to the house reef.

Each bungalow fits up to two adults comfortably, with options to add an extra bed for small families or triple-share arrangements. Constructed from local Sulawesi hardwood, the natural air circulation keeps the interiors naturally cool alongside ceiling fans.
